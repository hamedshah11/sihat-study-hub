4.1 Functions of the skeleton

The skeleton gives the body its shape and supports soft tissue. It protects vital organs, like the brain inside the skull and the heart and lungs inside the ribcage, and gives muscles something to pull on.

Exam trap. In adults, blood cells are made in red marrow in flat bones and the ends of long bones. The shafts hold yellow marrow.
